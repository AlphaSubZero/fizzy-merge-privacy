Privacy Policy for Fizzy Merge: Cocktail Drop

Package ID: com.fizzymerge
Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fizzy Merge: Cocktail Drop ("the App") handles user information. Your privacy is important to us: the App is designed to work completely offline and does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal information from users. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials;
  • Location data, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data;
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, or usage analytics;
  • Crash logs or telemetry sent to external servers.

The App does not request internet access or any sensitive Android permissions.

2. Local Data Storage

To provide basic gameplay convenience, the App saves only the following non-personal preferences locally on your device using standard Android storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Your local high score (best table score);
  • Your background music on/off setting.

This data never leaves your device and is automatically removed if you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Fizzy Merge: Cocktail Drop does not display third-party advertisements, does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not share any data with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future updates introduce new features (such as leaderboards, cloud saves, or advertisements), this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before those changes take effect. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated effective date.
